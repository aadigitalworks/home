Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a website cost in Wexford?
Our websites start from €200 for a landing page and go up to €900+ for full e-commerce. Final price depends on your specific needs.
What is included in the €200 website?
The Starter Landing Page includes a single high-converting page, the ability to connect your own domain (or purchase one separately), free hosting on GitHub Pages, and mobile responsiveness.
Can I see a free website preview before paying?
Yes! We create a free preview/concept of your website. You review it, and only if you love it, we proceed with the final build.
Do I need to buy my own domain?
You can connect an existing domain you already own, or we can help you purchase a new one (domain cost is separate from our packages).
What hosting do you provide?
For static websites (landing pages, business sites), we use free GitHub Pages hosting. For e-commerce websites that require databases and payment processing, we recommend and set up paid hosting solutions.
Can you connect professional email to my phone?
Absolutely. We set up professional email addresses (Zoho Mail) and configure them on your smartphone.
Do you work with businesses outside Wexford?
Yes, we serve clients across Ireland
