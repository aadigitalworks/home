Web Design Wexford FAQs

How much does a website cost in Wexford?
Our websites start from €200 for a landing page and go up to €900+ for full e-commerce. Final price depends on your specific needs. All packages include a free preview so you can see your website before you pay.
What is included in the €200 website?
The Starter Landing Page includes a single high-converting page, the ability to connect your own domain (or purchase one separately), free hosting on GitHub Pages, and mobile responsiveness. It's perfect for tradespeople, consultants and startups in Wexford.
Can I see a free website preview before paying?
Yes! We create a free preview/concept of your website. You review it, and only if you love it, we proceed with the final build. There's no payment required for the preview and no obligation to proceed.
Do I need to buy my own domain?
You can connect an existing domain you already own, or we can help you purchase a new one (domain cost is separate from our packages). We recommend .ie domains for Wexford businesses targeting local customers.
What hosting do you provide?
For static websites (landing pages, business sites), we use free GitHub Pages hosting. For e-commerce websites that require databases and payment processing, we recommend and set up paid hosting solutions with providers like Vercel, Netlify or traditional cPanel hosting.
Can you connect professional email to my phone?
Absolutely. We set up professional email addresses (Zoho Mail) and configure them on your smartphone, tablet and desktop email clients. This gives your Wexford business a professional appearance with branded email addresses like info@yourbusiness.ie.
Do you work with businesses outside Wexford?
Yes, we serve clients across Ireland including Dublin, Carlow, Waterford, Wicklow and Westmeath. However, we specialise in serving Wexford businesses and understand the local market in Wexford Town, Enniscorthy, Gorey, New Ross, Bunclody and Rosslare.
How long does it take to build a website?
A typical landing page takes 3-5 business days. A business website with 3-5 pages takes 1-2 weeks. E-commerce websites take 2-4 weeks depending on the number of products and complexity. We'll give you a clear timeline before we start.
Will my website be mobile-friendly?
Yes, every website we build is mobile-first and fully responsive. Over 60% of web traffic in Ireland comes from mobile devices, so we design for smartphones and tablets first, then scale up to desktop.
Do you offer SEO services in Wexford?
Yes. All our websites are built SEO-ready with clean code, fast loading speeds, schema markup and mobile optimization. We also offer dedicated local SEO services to help Wexford businesses rank higher in Google Search and Google Maps.
